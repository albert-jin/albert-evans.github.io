This work tackles one of the most pressing challenges in deploying LLM-based multi-agent systems: the daunting complexity of manually tuning agent roles, communication protocols, and collaboration strategies. OptiMAS introduces a fully automated optimization pipeline that treats the entire multi-agent system as a differentiable computational graph, where agent prompts, interaction patterns, and even the number of agents themselves are jointly optimized through a novel combination of gradient-based search and reinforcement learning. I am immensely proud of this achievement and eagerly anticipate presenting it to the EMNLP audience in Budapest! 🎊🇭🇺
