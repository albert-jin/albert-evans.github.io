Our work introduces a radically different approach to in-context learning that shifts the paradigm from example memorization to context compilation. Unlike standard ICL methods that simply concatenate demonstrations into the context window, our CCA framework actively compiles the provided demonstrations into compact, structured representations that capture underlying task patterns and decision boundaries. This work opens up exciting new directions for efficient and principled in-context learning, and I am deeply thankful to our collaborators at CUHK for making this impactful research possible.
