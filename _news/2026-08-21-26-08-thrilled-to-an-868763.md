This paper introduces a novel adaptive reasoning framework that dynamically balances continuous-space (soft token) reasoning and discrete-space (symbolic) reasoning within a single LLM. Rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all reasoning path, HybridThink employs a lightweight learned router that decides the optimal reasoning mode on a per-step basis, leveraging the rich representational power of continuous vectors for nuanced mathematical or spatial tasks, while seamlessly switching to discrete symbols for rigorous logical deduction and rule-based operations.
This work represents a significant step toward more human-like, flexible reasoning in LLMs, and I am incredibly excited to share our findings with the community at the conference in Budapest! 🥂🇭🇺
