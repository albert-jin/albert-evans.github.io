This research tackles a fundamental yet previously overlooked geometric pathology in continuous-space reasoning: we discover that soft tokens generated by LLMs during chain-of-thought processes tend to drift off the natural data manifold, leading to degraded semantic coherence and reasoning failures. To address this, we propose a lightweight geometric correction mechanism that dynamically projects off-manifold soft token representations back onto the learned manifold during the reasoning trajectory, effectively regularizing the continuous reasoning space without modifying the underlying model weights. I eagerly look forward to presenting this work and discussing its implications with the research community in Budapest! 🔥🇭🇺
