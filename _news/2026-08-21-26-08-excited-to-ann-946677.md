Our work reinterprets multi-agent prompt optimization through the lens of information contracting, a completely fresh perspective that departs from conventional gradient-based or evolutionary approaches. We introduce BID-SPO, a bidding-based framework where each agent in a multi-agent system bids for information exchange opportunities, and the contribution of each bid is fairly evaluated via Shapley values computed on the fly.
This work bridges game theory, information theory, and multi-agent LLM systems, offering both theoretical insights and practical tools for building more capable and efficient multi-agent AI systems. I am deeply grateful to our collaborators at CUHK for their invaluable contributions, and I cannot wait to share this exciting work with the community in Budapest! 💪🇭🇺
