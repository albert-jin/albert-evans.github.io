It was a great honor to be invited as a guest speaker for the Ph.D. Entrance Education Thematic Class Series organized by the Faculty of Electronic and Information Engineering (EI) at Xi’an Jiaotong University. The sessions were held in mid-September 2024 across various colleges within the faculty, including Information and Communication Engineering, at the Innovation Harbour Campus (IHP).

As a Ph.D. representative from the School of Information and Communication Engineering, I was delighted to share my practical experience with the incoming 2024 Ph.D. cohort, focusing on how to enhance research efficiency using digital tools.

It was a truly meaningful opportunity to give back to our academic community by helping fellow students navigate the challenges of a Ph.D. life. I sincerely hope my sharing provided them with practical, actionable insights to kickstart their research.

The thematic class series was successfully held, and I look forward to seeing the new doctoral cohort thrive in their future academic endeavors, guided by the spirit of the "Westward Relocation" of XJTU.
