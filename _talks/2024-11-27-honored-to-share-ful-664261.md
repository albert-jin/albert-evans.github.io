It was a great honor to be invited as a keynote speaker at the “Passing on the Torch, Illuminating the Research Path” Academic Exchange and Sharing Session, organized by the Third Doctorial Party Branch of the School of Information and Communication Engineering under the Faculty of Electronic and Information Engineering (EI) at Xi’an Jiaotong University.

The event was successfully held on November 27, 2024, at 18:30, in Room 4-7222, Hongli Building, Innovation Harbour Campus (IHP) . As a recipient of the 2024 National Scholarship for Doctoral Students, I was delighted to share my practical research experiences with fellow students. My presentation, under the theme of “Research Experience Sharing,” provided a detailed guide to the full research workflow.

It was a truly meaningful opportunity to give back to our academic community by helping my peers enhance their research skills. I sincerely hope that my sharing provided practical insights that will support them in their own research journeys.

I look forward to seeing more such academic exchange activities in our faculty, fostering innovation and collaboration.
