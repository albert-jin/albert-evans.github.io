Xi’an, China – December 9, 2025 – It was a great honor to be invited as a guest speaker at the 37th “Tengfei Cup” Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition Information Session, jointly organized by the Faculty of Electronic and Information Engineering (EI) and the School of Economics and Finance at Xi’an Jiaotong University.
The event was held at Room 5-1E107, Hanying Building, Innovation Harbour Campus (IHP) , and aimed to clarify competition rules, enhance project quality, and promote interdisciplinary collaboration between the two schools.

In my session as a Ph.D. student from the Faculty of EI, I shared practical experiences and strategies for competition success. The enthusiastic participation and thoughtful questions from the attending students once again proved the strong innovative spirit at XJTU. I sincerely hope my sharing helped fellow students better prepare for the competition and encouraged more cross-disciplinary collaboration. This event was a meaningful opportunity for me to give back to the university community. 

I look forward to seeing outstanding projects from this year’s “Tengfei Cup” and wish all participants great success.
